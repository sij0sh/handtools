**Core:** Instructions are guilty of redundancy until they demonstrate unique value.

**Assume model competence.** Omit generic practices, obvious implications, and instructions already enforced elsewhere. Instruct the agent on what is unique to the task, constraints, or desired reasoning.

**Lead with the job.** State what the agent must accomplish, not what the instructions contain.

**Establish the mental model.** Prefer one organizing concept that explains the workflow over enumerating individual actions.

**Write behavioral rules.** Use direct, natural imperatives. Describe decisions and outcomes rather than administrative machinery.
